Two figures that need checking before you present them

Monthly active users (~60M) and share of the online apparel market (30–35%) both circulate widely but trace back to 2023 secondary reporting rather than a filing. They are the weakest numbers on the slide. Either date them explicitly as 2023, or drop them — the FY25 figures above carry the argument on their own.

The point of the slide

Why the revenue mix matters more than the totals

Nearly half of Myntra's revenue is logistics — ₹2,919 Cr of ₹6,043 Cr. That makes a return a paid event, not a neutral one.

Which matters here because five of seven interviewees have bought two items intending to send one back. Every one of those is a comparison the product declined to host, paid for twice in reverse logistics. A feature that resolves the comparison before checkout is margin-accretive even before it converts anyone.

Terms

Defined, so the slide can use them

EBITDAEarnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation — roughly, operating profit before financing and accounting effects
Revenue from operationsIncome from the core business, excluding other income such as interest earned
Marketplace revenueCommission Myntra takes on goods sold by third-party sellers, rather than the value of the goods
GMVGross merchandise value — the total value of goods sold through the platform. Not revenue
MAUMonthly active users
Wishlist conversionAs defined for this project: the share of users who buy at least one saved item within 30 days of saving it. Per user, not per item
DRRDecision resolution rate — see Part 6
RICEReach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. A prioritisation score, not a measurement
Competitive

What Myntra already ships

Checked directly in the app, because two directions were abandoned on the strength of it:

  • Fit Assist — recommends a size from order history. Killed a fit-confidence MVP that had already been built and deployed.
  • Price-drop alerts on wishlisted items — killed a price-certainty direction before it was built.
  • Collections — manual folders inside the wishlist. Not competition: 6 of 7 interviewees have never used one.
  • No comparison capability of any kind in the wishlist.

A third-party industry exists purely to watch Myntra wishlists for price and stock changes — Flipshope, GlassIt, PriceHistory, TrackMonkey among them. People install external software to make the feature work, which is its own evidence of what the wishlist does not do.
